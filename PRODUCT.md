# Product

## Register

product

## Users

All roles use this app regularly, from the same session:

- **Solicitantes** — create and track indirect contracting requests
- **Directores de Proyecto** — review and approve requests for their frentes
- **Contratos** (tramite + minuta) — advance requests through the contracting workflow
- **Controles / Director de Controles** — register ADPRO codes, give final approval
- **Admin** — configure users, approvers, and frentes

Context: a mix of office-based desk work and field access (phones, tablets on site). No single "primary" user — all roles depend on the app to do their job. The workflow is sequential and role-gated; missing a step blocks everyone downstream.

## Product Purpose

Manage the full lifecycle of indirect contracting requests (órdenes de servicio, contratos, otrosi) for the Baia Kristal construction project. Requests are created, routed through a multi-step approval chain, and tracked to completion. Success is a complete, auditable contracting record with zero lost requests and no manual follow-up needed.

## Brand Personality

Confiable · Claro · Profesional

Voice: direct, unambiguous, institutional without being cold. This is a tool people use to do real work under real deadlines. Confidence is earned through precision, not decoration. Errors speak plainly; labels say exactly what they mean.

## Anti-references

- **Consumer SaaS** (Notion, Linear, Loom): playful type, large whitespace, personality-first UI. This audience needs density and certainty, not delight.
- **Generic Bootstrap / off-the-shelf Material**: the standard "internal tools" look — navy header, gray table rows, blue pill buttons. That aesthetic signals no investment in the experience.
- **Enterprise bloat** (SAP, Oracle): panel-within-panel navigation, 12-column data grids everywhere, menus that require training to read.

## Design Principles

1. **The workflow is the interface.** Every screen exists to move a request forward or show where it stands. Navigation, hierarchy, and emphasis should always reflect the current step, not just the data.
2. **Legibility under pressure.** Users check status on a phone mid-meeting. Labels, states, and actions must be unmistakable at a glance — no cryptic icons, no hover-only affordances.
3. **Trust through consistency.** The same action always looks the same. The same state always shows the same badge. Predictability builds confidence; surprises break it.
4. **Density without clutter.** Lists of requests can be long. Pack information efficiently, but never sacrifice scanability for density.
5. **Mobile is a first-class citizen.** Field staff and directors access from phones. Every critical workflow path must be completable without a desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum for contrast, focus order, and form labeling
- Mobile-first responsive layout — critical actions completable on phones (320px+)
- Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 44px)
- Spanish-language (Colombia locale); dates and currency formatted accordingly
- Light mode only (dark mode explicitly disabled by the project)
